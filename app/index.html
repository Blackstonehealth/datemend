Datemend preview v0.1

Put capture dates, locations and captions back on the photos and videos in your Snapchat data export. Everything runs in this browser tab.

Your files never leave your computer. This page makes no network requests while you use it (its security policy blocks them). Your export is read by your browser and the result is saved on your device. You can switch off your internet connection after the page loads and it will still work. This tool never opens the download links inside your export and never connects to Snapchat.
Free preview: this tool has been tested only on sample exports built from third-party reports of the format, not on a real, current Snapchat export. Snapchat can change its export at any time. Keep your original export until you have checked the result, and read the report the tool produces.
